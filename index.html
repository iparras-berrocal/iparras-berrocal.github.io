Research perspective

“Mediterranean climate impacts emerge from the interaction of oceanic and atmospheric processes across multiple scales. Understanding these interactions requires combining observations, regional climate simulations and impact-oriented climate information within a common framework.”

My research focuses on the Mediterranean Sea, where ocean–atmosphere interactions, regional climate dynamics and coastal processes shape some of the most climate-sensitive regions in the world.

Research focus

Three connected lines of inquiry

01

Regional climate modelling

Developing and evaluating regional climate simulations to better understand Mediterranean climate variability and future change.

Med-CORDEX · RCSMs · Ensembles
02

Mediterranean ocean change

Understanding how circulation, sea level, stratification and deep-water formation respond to climate change.

Circulation · Sea level · Water masses
03

Climate impacts and services

Translating climate information into impact-relevant indicators and climate services supporting adaptation.

Climatic Impact Drivers · Coastal impacts · Adaptation
